about us

Slow photography, built on light

lumen studio. began in a rented room with one light and a stubborn belief that restraint beats noise. A decade later we still shoot the same way: fewer frames, better decisions.

We work with brands, publications and families who want images with staying power — pictures that feel like they were taken on purpose.
